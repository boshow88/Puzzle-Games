How to play

  • Click a cell to cycle through empty → ☀ → ☾. Pre-filled cells are locked.
  • Each row and column must contain half suns and half moons, and never three of the same in a row.
  • The badges between cells are constraints: = means the two neighbours must match, × means they must differ.
  • The puzzle is solved when every cell is filled and no rule is broken.
