How to play

  • A bridge is a straight line (horizontal or vertical) joining two islands. Each island’s number is how many bridges connect to it. A pair of islands takes at most two bridges, and bridges can’t cross another bridge or an island.
  • In Build mode, drag from one island to a neighbour or click the gap between two. Left cycles up (0 → 1 → 2 → 0), right cycles down.
  • Switch to Mark mode to note what you’re sure of — click a corridor to lock its count (0, 1 or 2) or click an island to flag it handled. Marks are just reminders and don’t change the puzzle.
  • Solved when every island’s number is satisfied and all islands form a single connected network.
