How to play

  • Each island’s number is how many bridges connect to it. Bridges run straight (horizontal or vertical), at most two between a pair, and never cross.
  • Build mode: drag island to island, or click the gap between two. Left adds a bridge (0 → 1 → 2 → 0), right reverses.
  • Mark mode: click a corridor to confirm its count, or a full island to mark it done (locking its connections). Marks are just aids and don’t affect the puzzle.
  • Solved when every island’s number is met and all islands form one connected network.
