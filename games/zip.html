How to play

  • Drag from the cell marked 1 to draw a path through every white cell.
  • Visit the numbered checkpoints in order — 1 → 2 → 3 → … → N.
  • The path cannot cross black walls or enter grey holes; only orthogonal moves.
  • You can pick up the drag from any cell already on the path — everything after that cell is dropped.
  • Solved when the path covers every white cell with checkpoints in the right order.
