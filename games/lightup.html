How to play

  • Post a on a floor cell to watch its whole row and column — its view runs until a wall (pillar) or the edge.
  • The goal is to have every floor cell watched. No two may see each other, and a numbered pillar must have exactly that many in the cells directly next to it — up, down, left and right.
  • Pick an input mode — cycle, guard, mark, erase — then tap or drag. is just your own "no guard here" note.
