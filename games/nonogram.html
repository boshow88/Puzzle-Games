How to play

  • Each clue number is a run of that many consecutive cells, in the listed order (left→right for rows, top→bottom for columns), with at least one gap between runs.
  • Pick a brush — fill, mark, or clear — then tap or drag cells to paint that state. just flags a cell you’ve ruled out.
  • Solved when the cells match every row and column clue.
