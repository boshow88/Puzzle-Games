Tango generator stats

Runs N puzzles per (size, difficulty) combination and reports per-tier solver-walk counts plus the carve's hardness score. Tier definitions (chain length measured by backward closure — only the L1 placements actually needed for the contradiction count): L1 = direct rules; L2 = single-line hypothesis, chain = 1; L3 = single-line hypothesis, chain ≥ 2; L4 = anywhere-on-board hypothesis, chain ≤ 5 (with a wall-chain discount: any maximal connected run of T-wall placements of size K inside the chain is counted as ceil(K/3), since consecutive walls are visually mechanical). Difficulty: Easy = L1+L2, Medium = +L3, Hard = +L4. L1 splits into L1w cells (T-wall — `=` / `×` to a known neighbour) and L1o cells (T-count / T-three). T-wall is weighted 0.5 in hard; the rest follow Σ L1o·1 + L2·1.5 + L3·N/2 + L4-score. L4 score is the sum of log10(E)·N over the L4 steps (E = empty count at the moment the step fires). After K-selection, Medium / Hard run a cluster-simplification pass that absorbs prefill-adjacent walls (turning the trivial T-wall step into a given). simp s is the average per- puzzle intensity (sampled U(0.3, 1) for Hard, U(0.2, 0.8) for Medium); simp abs is the average # walls absorbed; simp red is the average # redundant walls (both sides prefilled) swept unconditionally. For Hard we also walk the solve trace and report how many L4 deductions actually used the wall-chain discount (rawChainLength > chainLength): L4 disc avg = avg discounted L4 steps per puzzle, disc K = puzzles with ≥1 such step, max save = largest single-step savings. unverified shows how many puzzles in the sample ultimately ended up unverified. attempts is the average K (carves actually run); easy is always 1.
