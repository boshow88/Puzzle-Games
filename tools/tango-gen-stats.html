Tango generator stats

Runs N puzzles per (size, difficulty) combination and reports average prefill count, wall count, tier-tagged erasures, and how many cells each tier leaves unsolved. Tier definitions: L1 = direct rules; L2 = hypothesise + 1 in-line L1 step; L3 = hypothesise + 2+ in-line L1 steps; L4 = L3 with one perpendicular layer unlocked (each cell placed on the primary line opens its perpendicular line for further L1 propagation). Difficulty: Easy = L1+L2, Medium = +L3, Hard = +L4. Open the browser console for self-check warnings.
